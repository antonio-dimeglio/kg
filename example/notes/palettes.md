# Palettes

kg has five colour palettes, each in a light and a dark mode.

They are Porcelain, Mist, Garden, Sorbet and Ink. The choice is kept in the browser, so it applies to every project and is not part of the map. The same colours are used for cards, groups and Mermaid diagrams. Group colours in a canvas are the numbers 1 to 6 of the JSON Canvas format, and each palette gives them its own shades; see [[canvas-format]].
